	SubTypeTime Start/End: Sub 1 minute, 2, 3, 4, 5, 10, 20, Other

	- are there prop blocks?

	- Does the scenary have lava?
	- Does the scenary have acid/goo/or other liquid?
	- Does the scenary feature trees?

Tech <Box Tech>

LJ Block: Yes/No/Specific
Ladder Room: Yes/No

Path <Linear | Circular | Spiral | Staged | Locked>
	- Linear: The map has a clear start and end point that are not near each other
	- Circular: The start of the map is near the end of the map
	- Spiral: The map sometimes gets close the the start, or gets close to a previous part of the map, one fall could set you back.
	- Staged: The map has different sections with different styles.
	- Locked: Some sections require keys and specific area to reach before continuing.

Special Blocks/Sections (Boosters | Wall Jumps | Jump Pads | Water | Slides | Gravity | Turbines | Crouch)
	- Boosters give lots of horizontal speed
	- Wall Jumps like as seen in kz_rise
	- Jump Pads give lots of vertical speed
	- Water is there a water section?
	- Slides are blocks which have zero friction
	- Turbines are sections with wind that push you.
	- Is there a forced crouch section? (Vent)

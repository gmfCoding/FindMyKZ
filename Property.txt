Scenary tags (multi-select, grouped):
	Setting: Confined | Indoors | Outdoors | Outerspace
	Biomes & Nature: Beach | Water | Lava | Acid | Trees | Desert | Forest | Mountain | Snowy | Swamp | Cave | Jungle | Tundra
	Urban & Architecture: Industrial | Cityscape | Ruins | Cyberpunk | Medieval | Post-Apocalyptic | Suburban | Underground | Abstract
	Art Style: Realistic | Low Poly | Futuristic
	Climate & Tone: Warm | Neutral | Cold
	Atmosphere & Lighting: Daytime | Nighttime | Sunset | Foggy | Spooky | Surreal | Neon | Monochrome
	Gameplay & Hazards: Toxic | Void | Ice | Floating Islands
	- Setting: how enclosed/open the playable space is, and whether it is on another world.
	- Biomes & Nature: natural environment, terrain and liquids present.
	- Urban & Architecture: built environment, era and architectural style.
	- Art Style: how realistic or stylised the visuals are.
	- Climate & Tone: overall warm/cold/neutral mood or climate.
	- Atmosphere & Lighting: time-of-day lighting, weather mood and colour treatment.
	- Gameplay & Hazards: hazardous or unusual environment elements.
	- Confined: not much space in the map.
	- Indoors: has a reachable indoor section.
	- Outdoors: has a reachable outdoor section.
	- Industrial: has an industrial look.
	- Beach: features a beach.
	- Water/Lava/Acid: features that liquid.
	- Trees: features trees.
	- Realistic: photorealistic or grounded art style.
	- Low Poly: deliberately simple/faceted geometry.
	- Outerspace: set in space or on another world.
	- Futuristic: sci-fi architecture/technology.
	- Warm/Neutral/Cold: colour palette or climate.

Path <Linear | Circular | Spiral | Staged | Locked>
	- Linear: The map has a clear start and end point that are not near each other
	- Circular: The start of the map is near the end of the map
	- Spiral: The map sometimes gets close the the start, or gets close to a previous part of the map, one fall could set you back.
	- Staged: The map has different sections with different styles.
	- Locked: Some sections require keys and specific area to reach before continuing.
